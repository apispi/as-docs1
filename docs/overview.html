Platform Overview

ApiSpi is an agentic AI platform that deploys production-ready AI agents for government, enterprise, and professional services teams across Australia.

Core Offerings

CategoryDescription
AI AgentsPre-built autonomous agents for specific business functions, available via monthly subscription.
Digital AvatarsAI-powered video personas for client-facing professional services.
TrainingHands-on workshops and online courses covering AI adoption for teams and leaders.
AriaAI assistant embedded in the platform, accessible to all authenticated users.

Application Structure

The platform is a Laravel web application with three distinct areas:

  • Public website — Aria chatbot widget, agent catalogue, checkout, and lead capture.
  • Authenticated dashboard — Access to subscribed agents, connector management, full Aria assistant, and training.
  • Admin panel — Manage users, subscriptions, agents, connectors, and content.
