Partner Program

Two partnership types for agencies, consultants, and referrers.

Partner Types

Referral Partner

Earn up to 20% recurring revenue share by referring clients to ApiSpi. No delivery required — refer and earn.

Agency Partner

For digital and marketing agencies that deliver AI solutions to their own clients. Includes:

  • Agency-tier pricing.
  • Volume discounts.
  • Joint go-to-market support.
  • Co-sell support.

Applying

Partners apply at /partners. The application is handled by PartnerController and a notification is sent to the admin team. Onboarding takes 48 hours from approval.

The partner application route is throttled to 5 submissions per 10 minutes per IP.

vs. the In-App Affiliate Program

This formal Partner Program (application-based, up to 20% revenue share) is separate from the automatic, self-service Affiliate Program every user gets via their dashboard referral link — the latter pays a fixed commission rate on referred purchases with no application required.
