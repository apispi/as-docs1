Getting Started

From zero to a working AI workspace in about ten minutes. Follow the steps in order — each one leaves you with something you can use immediately, and none requires technical setup until the optional final step.

What you'll need: just an email address. Every new account starts a 7-day free trial — no credit card required to begin, and starter connectors (news, weather, world time) are pre-connected so Aria is useful from your very first message.

1. Create Your Account

  1. Go to apispi.com/register
  2. Enter your name, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters)
  3. You're logged in automatically and land on your dashboard

Look for the thought-bubble callouts around the dashboard — they point at your recommended next step and are clickable. You can dismiss any of them.

2. Say Hello to Aria

Aria is your AI assistant and the fastest way to see ApiSpi working.

  1. Open Aria from the sidebar (/dashboard/aria)
  2. Try a question that uses your starter connectors:
    • "What's the weather in Sydney this weekend?"
    • "Give me this morning's top news headlines."
    • "What time is it in London right now?"
  3. Ask "What model are you using?" any time — Aria answers honestly about its current provider, model, and mode

Aria knows your local date, time, and saved location, so "today" and "nearby" work the way you'd expect. Set your location and preferences under Profile → Personalisation.

3. Complete Your Profile

Two minutes here makes every future answer better. Go to /dashboard/profile → Personalisation and set:

SettingWhy it matters
Preferred name & job titleAria addresses you properly and frames answers for your role.
LocationAnchors weather, news, times — and the timezone your schedules run in.
Communication style & response lengthFormal or casual, concise or detailed — your call.
Custom instructionsStanding instructions Aria always follows.

4. Connect Your Tools

Connectors let Aria and your agents act on your real accounts — read email, check your calendar, query your CRM. Browse the catalogue at /dashboard/connectors.

Two kinds of connection

TypeHow it connectsExamples
OAuthClick Connect, approve on the provider's own consent page, done. ApiSpi never sees your password.Gmail, Microsoft 365, Xero, LinkedIn
API keyPaste a key from the provider into the connector's form.Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, Twilio

Recommended first connections

  1. Your email — Gmail or Outlook (OAuth). Then try: "Summarise my unread email."
  2. An AI provider of your own (optional) — bring your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or other key and Aria uses it instead of platform credit. See the full provider list on the Connectors page.
Worried about what a connector can do? Every connector's tool list is shown honestly on its catalogue card, you can disable individual tools without disconnecting, and everything is governed and logged — see Governance How-To.

5. Pick Your Agents

Agents are pre-built specialists — bid writing, security compliance, bookkeeping, customer support, and more (full catalogue).

  1. Browse /dashboard/catalog and open any agent to see its skills and use cases
  2. Your subscription is a pick-3 platform plan: one subscription, three agent slots you can activate agents into
  3. Have a promo code? Apply it on the payment page — the Stripe button updates to the discounted amount

6. Automate Something

My Schedule runs any saved Prompt automatically — a morning briefing is the classic first schedule:

  1. In Aria, refine a prompt you like (e.g. "Top 5 news stories for my industry, plus today's weather and my calendar") and save it to My Prompts
  2. Go to My Schedule (/dashboard/schedule) → create a schedule → pick your Prompt and a time
  3. Times run in your profile location's timezone; results arrive in your Aria chat, badged as scheduled
  4. Use Run now to test it immediately, and the schedule's own page to review run history

7. Set Your Guardrails (Optional)

Under /dashboard/profile → Governance you can add personal blocked keywords, a daily token budget, country-based sovereignty rules, and guardrail alerts. If you're part of an organisation, your admin's policy applies automatically and your settings can only tighten it. Full walkthroughs: Governance How-To.

8. Go Beyond the Dashboard (Optional)

Everything Aria can do is also available programmatically through the LLM Gateway:

Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely cause & fix
Aria gives generic answers, no live dataNo AI provider or connector is active. Check /dashboard/connectors — starter connectors should be listed as active; connect a provider key for full capability.
"Limit reached" messageYou've hit your daily token limit (yours or your organisation's). It resets next day, or raise your personal limit under Governance → Limits.
A tool won't run / is missingIt may be disabled on the connector, blocked by mode restrictions, or governed by org policy — Aria's reply explains which. See Governance.
Schedule ran at the wrong timeSchedules use your profile location's timezone — set your location under Profile → Personalisation.
Trial expiredYou'll be routed to the payment page; pick your plan (and apply any promo code) to continue.
