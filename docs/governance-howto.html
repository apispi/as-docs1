Governance How-To

Practical guides for setting up your personal AI governance controls. Two layers apply to every conversation: an org-wide policy managed by your organisation's ApiSpi administrator, and your own overrides on the Governance tab of /dashboard/profile. Your rules can only add to or tighten the org policy — never loosen it.

Block Keywords

Prevent discussions on specific topics by adding blocked keywords.

  1. Go to /dashboard/profile → Governance tab → Guardrails sub-tab
  2. Add your personal blocked keywords (these stack on top of the org policy — you can only add, not remove)
  3. Choose your guardrail action: block (reject), redact (mask), or warn (proceed with warning) — it applies to your overrides
Keywords are case-insensitive by default. Enable whole word matching to prevent partial matches (e.g., "cat" won't match "category").

Set Daily Token Limits

Cap how many tokens you can consume per day to control costs.

  1. Go to /dashboard/profile → Governance tab → Limits sub-tab
  2. Set a personal daily limit — if your organisation also sets one, the stricter (lower) limit wins

Once the limit is reached, further Aria requests get a fixed "limit reached" message instead of an AI response until the next day.

Block Connectors by Country

Restrict connectors from specific countries for data-sovereignty compliance.

  1. Go to /dashboard/profile → Governance tab → Sovereignty sub-tab
  2. Add country codes to your block list (merged with any org-wide list)

Connectors whose country field matches a blocked country are removed from Aria's candidate tool set before the model ever sees them — model providers carry country labels for exactly this purpose (see Connectors).

Receive Guardrail Alerts

Get notified when your guardrails are triggered.

  1. Go to /dashboard/profile → Governance tab → Guardrails sub-tab
  2. Enable Guardrail alerts for in-app and/or email notifications

Alerts fire on keyword matches, injection attempts, and output-moderation flags.

Review Guardrail Activity

View your own guardrail events at /dashboard/profile → Governance tab (shows recent blocks, injections, warnings).

Good to Know

  • Governance applies to every account — no plan gating on enforcement.
  • Policy free-text notes accept up to 50,000 characters.
  • Saving from a governance sub-tab keeps you on that sub-tab.
  • Guardrails (blocked keywords, injection detection, output scanning) are enforced on all API gateway paths, not just Aria chat.
  • Your organisation may additionally enforce a connector kill switch, tool-access restrictions (read-only/approval modes), access windows, or an emergency stop. When one of these blocks something, Aria explains the restriction in its reply; contact your administrator to change org-wide settings.
  • Organisations running the On-Prem Appliance enforce these same controls at a network choke point — all LLM and agent traffic passes through the governed gateway, with direct egress to AI providers blocked at the perimeter.
