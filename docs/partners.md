# Partner Program

ApiSpi offers two partnership types for agencies, consultants, and referrers.

## Partner Types

### Referral Partner
Earn up to **20% recurring revenue share** by referring clients to ApiSpi. No delivery required — just refer and earn.

### Agency Partner
For digital and marketing agencies that deliver AI solutions to their own clients. Includes:
- Agency-tier pricing
- Volume discounts
- Joint go-to-market support
- Co-sell support

## Applying

Partners apply at `/partners`. The application form is handled by `PartnerController` and sends a notification to `admin@apispi.com`. Onboarding takes 48 hours from approval.

The partner application route is throttled to **5 submissions per 10 minutes** per IP.
