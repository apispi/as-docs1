FAQs

Does it work with Xero, MYOB, and QuickBooks?

Yes. Native integrations with Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, and Reckon. Import/export via CSV for other software.

Can it prepare STP and BAS?

Absolutely. Generate STP payroll reports and BAS calculations with work papers ready for your BAS agent.

How accurate is the AI categorisation?

Initial accuracy is typically 85-90%. The AI learns from your corrections and improves over time.
