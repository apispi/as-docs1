Digital Plumber

A virtual back-office for plumbing and trades businesses. It triages customer jobs, estimates quotes, schedules and dispatches work, advises on standards and compliance, and raises invoices — turning enquiries into booked, billed jobs.

FAQs

Does it replace a licensed plumber?

No. It handles quoting, scheduling, and admin and offers standards guidance, but it flags any work that legally requires a licensed plumber and a certificate of compliance.

How does it quote and invoice?

It estimates labour and materials into an itemised quote, and after the job raises an invoice through your connected accounting (Xero) with payment via Stripe.

Can customers book through it?

Yes. Connected to Twilio and your calendar, it takes job requests by call or SMS, books a time, and sends confirmations and reminders.
