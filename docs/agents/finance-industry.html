FAQs

Does it comply with financial regulations?

Yes. It generates reports aligned to ASIC, APRA, SEC, and MiFID II requirements. Always verify outputs against current regulatory guidance.

Can it analyse real-time market data?

It can process and synthesise market data you provide. Direct market data integration via API is available on enterprise plans.

Is our financial data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We maintain SOC 2 Type II certification and never use your data for model training.
