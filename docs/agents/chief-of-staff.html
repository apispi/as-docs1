FAQs

Does it send emails on my behalf?

By default it drafts replies for your review rather than sending automatically — you stay the final approver. Auto-send for routine correspondence can be enabled per governance policy.

How does it know what's a priority?

It learns from your calendar, inbox, and the action items you confirm, weighting deadlines, stakeholder seniority, and items you've flagged as important.

Can it work across email and chat?

Yes — connected to Gmail, your calendar, and Microsoft Teams, it triages and tracks commitments across whichever channels your organisation actually uses.
