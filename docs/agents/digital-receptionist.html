Digital Receptionist

An always-on virtual receptionist that answers calls and messages, books appointments, routes enquiries, takes messages, and captures new leads — so nothing goes unanswered after hours or at the front desk.

At a Glance

CategoryCommunications & Front Office
Rating⭐ 4.85
Users160+
Built forClinics and allied health, salons and trades, professional services, small offices, after-hours reception

What It Does

  • Answers calls & SMS 24/7
  • Speak to it — voice transcription & spoken replies
  • Books and reschedules appointments
  • Routes and triages enquiries
  • Takes and relays messages
  • Captures and qualifies leads
  • Answers common questions instantly

What’s Included

  • Inbound call and SMS handling
  • Voice input (speech-to-text) and spoken replies (text-to-speech)
  • Live calendar appointment booking
  • Enquiry routing and escalation
  • Message taking and relay
  • Lead capture and qualification
  • FAQ and business-hours answers

FAQs

How does it answer calls and texts?

Through your connected Twilio number for voice and SMS. Customers call or text as normal and the agent responds in real time.

Which calendars can it book into?

Any connected calendar such as Google Calendar. It only offers genuinely free slots and writes confirmed bookings back to the calendar.

Can it escalate to a human?

Yes. You define which enquiries are urgent or out of scope, and the agent escalates by call, SMS, or email and logs the message.
