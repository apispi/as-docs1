A personal shopping assistant that searches products, compares prices across retailers, finds the best deals, builds your basket to a budget, and tracks orders and deliveries.

FAQs

Which stores can it shop?

Any retailer connected to your account — for example Woolworths and Shopify storefronts. Connect more from the Catalog to widen its reach.

Can it actually place orders?

It builds and prices your basket and can initiate checkout via connected payment (Stripe). Final purchase confirmation stays with you unless you enable hands-off checkout.

Does it respect my budget and preferences?

Yes. Give it a budget and any dietary, brand, or quality preferences and it builds the basket to match, explaining any trade-offs.

Activate Shopping Agent into one of your platform plan's agent slots from the dashboard catalogue (/dashboard/catalog). New to ApiSpi? Start with the Getting Started guide.
