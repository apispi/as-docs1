A management-consulting agent for market sizing, segmentation, and where-to-play strategy. Maps the market, triangulates TAM/SAM/SOM, and surfaces attractive, under-served white space.

FAQs

How does it size a market?

It uses both top-down and bottom-up methods, then triangulates the gap between them to produce a defensible TAM, SAM, and SOM.

What inputs does it need?

Market data, customer or category research, and any internal demand signal you can provide. It works with whatever you have and flags gaps.

What do I get out of it?

A market map, a triangulated size, and a ranked set of where-to-play options including suggested beachheads.
