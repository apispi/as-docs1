FAQs

Is it HIPAA compliant?

Yes. All patient data is processed in HIPAA-compliant environments with end-to-end encryption. No PHI is stored or used for training.

Can it assist with telehealth documentation?

Absolutely. Generate session notes, treatment plans, and follow-up communications for telehealth consultations.

Does it integrate with practice management systems?

Yes, it works with common formats and can integrate via API with most major practice management platforms.
